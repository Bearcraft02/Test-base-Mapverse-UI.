Questions

Is the mod free?

Yes. The mod is free, and so are many cosmetics. Some cosmetics cost Tokens, and part of every sale goes to the player who made them.

Do other players need the mod to see my cosmetics?

Yes. Friends with the mod see your look. Shared cosmetics need online-mode servers, or a proxy that forwards Mojang UUIDs.

Can friends join my server without the mod?

Yes. Anyone on Java Edition can join your server from the normal Multiplayer menu.

How do creators get paid?

When someone buys a creator's cosmetic, XX% of the sale goes to that creator in Tokens.

What are development builds?

Early builds that connect to MapVerse development services. Service URLs are in config/mapverse.json.

Which Java version do I need?
